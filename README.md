
# EmployIQ – Student Skill Assessment & Placement Management

An AI-supported business innovation project focused on improving student placement readiness through structured skill assessment, role-fit analysis, and targeted training recommendations.

# EmployIQ Project

## Project Overview

EmployIQ is a student skill assessment and placement management prototype designed for the Education Management domain. It demonstrates how a placement cell and students can use skill ratings, predefined job-role benchmarks, role matching, and training recommendations to understand placement readiness and plan improvement.

The project brings together three assignment components:
- Business research using Google NotebookLM.
- A customised business assistant using Google Gemini Gems.
- A mobile application prototype created using Google AI Studio.

The application screenshots show an offline prototype with illustrative sample data and predefined rules. The project does not claim a live AI integration or production deployment.

## Project Objectives

- Identify a practical business problem related to student placement readiness.
- Research the selected domain and gather relevant business insights.
- Design a specialised AI assistant to support the placement-readiness use case.
- Demonstrate skill-gap analysis against predefined role benchmarks.
- Present suitable job-role matches based on entered competency ratings.
- Recommend focused training activities to address identified gaps.
- Create a clear, professional mobile application prototype.
- Communicate the proposed business value, limitations, and future scope.

### 2. Custom Business Assistant – Gemini Gems

A customised Gemini assistant is included as the second component of the project. Its intended role is to support users in understanding skill gaps, considering role-fit results, and planning targeted preparation.

Expected assistant support:

- Explain skill-gap and role-fit results.
- Suggest practice and learning actions.
- Help prepare structured placement-readiness summaries.
- Ask for missing information and communicate limitations.

Gem share link: https://share.gemini.google/BQ0wDEkkrpj3

### 3. EmployIQ Application Prototype

The mobile application prototype demonstrates the placement-readiness workflow through the following screens and features:

- Welcome screen and workspace role selection.
- Placement-readiness summary.
- Skill-gap analysis against role benchmarks.
- Strengths and identified skill gaps.
- Suitable job-role matches ranked by fit.
- Training recommendations with estimated duration and weekly practice hours.

### 4. Skill-Gap Analysis

The application compares a student's entered skill ratings with predefined benchmarks for a selected job role.

Visible skill categories include:

- Technical Skills.
- Aptitude & Quant.
- Communication.
- Analytical Skills.
- Problem-Solving.

The report presents student scores, benchmark scores, and the size of the skill gap.

### 5. Suitable Job-Role Matches

The prototype displays job roles ranked by alignment with the entered competency ratings.

The supplied sample report shows:

- Cloud / DevOps Associate – 82% fit.
- Business Analyst – 76% fit.
- QA / Test Engineer – 75% fit.
- Software Developer – 74% fit (selected target role).

These are illustrative results shown in the prototype, not verified employment predictions.

### 6. Training Recommendations

Training recommendations are linked to identified skill gaps and include priority, duration, weekly practice hours, and suggested activities.

Examples visible in the screenshots:

- **Algorithmic & Debugging Mastery** – High Priority; 4 weeks; 5 hours/week.
- **Applied Stack & Portfolio Lab** – Recommended; 2 weeks; 4 hours/week.

Suggested activities include pattern-based problem solving, edge-case testing, explaining time and space trade-offs, mini-projects, core computer science practice, and SQL queries.

## Expected Business Value

The proposed workflow may help placement teams and students:

- Use a more structured approach to readiness assessment.
- Understand strengths and improvement areas more clearly.
- Prioritise training based on skill gaps.
- Explore alternative job roles aligned with current ratings.
- Improve placement-related discussions through a consistent report.

These benefits are expected outcomes of the concept and have not been measured through a live deployment.

## Limitations and Future Scope

### Current Limitations

- The application uses illustrative sample data and predefined rules.
- Role benchmarks and scoring methods require validation.
- Screenshots do not demonstrate a production backend or secure authentication.
- A live AI service integration is not established by the supplied screenshots.
- Role-fit percentages should be treated as guidance, not a guarantee of placement.

### Future Scope

- Validate skill benchmarks with faculty, placement officers, and employers.
- Add secure student profiles and assessment history.
- Introduce cohort-level placement readiness dashboards.
- Track skill improvement over time.
- Connect training recommendations to learning resources.
- Consider optional verified AI assistance with appropriate privacy and accuracy safeguards.
